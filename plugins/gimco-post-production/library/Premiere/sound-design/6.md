## 6. Sound rules

- Cue only a real visible state change (a card arrives, a number rolls, a result lands). Land it on the spoken onset or the settle frame, never mid-move.
- Timbre by event: appear -> small pop; confirmed -> short chime after a click; stamp/weighted landing -> soft low impact; screenshot -> one damped click; typing -> short uneven burst; counter -> sparse ticks; wrong option -> one dull low click (never a mocking buzzer); scene transition -> soft low landing or silence. Whooshes: soft and varied, only for real movement (graphic in/out, transition, camera move) in energetic reels; calm or medical explainers stay without them unless the editor asks.
- Fewer than you think: at most one accent per 0.4 s; a calm explainer well under one per second. Alternate two sounds of the same timbre for a series.
- Effects bus about 7 dB under the voice peak (9-10 if dense or with music); +2 dB quieter whenever the editor says it is loud. Music only if requested and licensed: 5-8 dB under the effects, ducked 3-5 dB under speech, silent under the hook and from the sign-off. Nothing at or after the sign-off; one second of clean air before the goodbye. Delivery loudness about -14 LUFS, true peak about -1 dBTP.
- **Music default depends on the piece (C14):** talking-head reel -> music only if requested and licensed (the rule above). Speaker-less ad or motion piece (no voice) -> licensed music is the base and the pacing follows it (see the montage-hub speaker-less path).
- You cannot hear: ask the editor to play a named passage on a phone speaker and earbuds and answer: is speech clear, are effects comfortable, what should change.
